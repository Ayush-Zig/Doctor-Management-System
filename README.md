Doctor Management System

The proposed system is to make an online web application for easily taking appointment of a patient see the schedule of doctors, so that everyone can get information about doctor's availability, time period, and send request to doctor for Appointment.
This project is aimed at developing an online application for patient to appointing doctors. Users have to logging in the system to be able to take appointment of a doctor.
Doctors have to logging to see his appointments. The proposed system could be accessed from any corner of the world on net.

Proposed System
1.	Patient Scheduling: Patients contact the doctor's office through phone, in-person, or by other means to request an appointment. They may need to provide their personal information.
2.	Booking Fee: Booking will be needed to pay by patient as per doctor and provide the fee screenshot.
3.	Appointment Confirmation: The doctor checks if he has any Appointments or not then booking receipt fee or payment screenshot submitted by user/patient then he fill the checkbox provided to confirm the booking.
4.	Consultation: The patient meets with the doctor for their scheduled appointment. During the consultation, the doctor evaluates the patient's condition, provides medical advice, and prescribes medications or treatment as necessary.
5.	Billing and Payment: After the consultation, the payment of consultancy will be done direct to the doctor, if applicable.
